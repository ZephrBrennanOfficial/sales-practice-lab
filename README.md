## 💡 Core Learning Philosophy

My approach to learning sales rests on four core principles:

1. **Deliberate Practice over Passive Consumption:** Reading sales books is useless without immediate application. Every chapter I read is translated into a script, cold email, or roleplay scenario.
2. **Brutal Self-Awareness:** The fastest way to grow is to audit my own execution. I review my mock calls for filler words, rushed transitions, closed-ended questions, and missed emotional cues.
3. **Psychology & Value First:** Cold outreach isn't about pitching features; it's about understanding prospect pain points, business economics, and human decision-making.
4. **Consistency over Intensity:** SDR success requires daily micro-disciplines—prospecting blocks, objection practice, and market research executed consistently day after day.
